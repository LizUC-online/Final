About Elizabeth's Coffee House

Elizabeth's coffee house was founded in 2026 by Elizabeth and Michael Johnson, with the goal of creating a coffee space for coffee lovers everywhere. Nestled in the heart of downtown Erie, Pa, Elizabeth's Coffee House offers convenience, delicious sips, and quality coffee at an affordable price! From college students, to tourists and daily commuters, Elizabeth's Coffee House has something for everyone. Our menu features seasonal items as well as classics, so you can find your perfect coffee craving. Stop in today and mention the code "Coffee Craze" for $2 off your purchase!
